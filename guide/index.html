Rabbit r1 · MagSafe + Qi charging case · rev A

Bunnycage

A three-part printed case that wraps the whole r1, snaps it onto any MagSafe mount and charges it wirelessly. The r1 has no charging coil of its own, so the case carries one and feeds the USB-C port from a bay on the left edge. A USB-C socket on the outside of that edge passes cable power and data straight through, so the r1 can stay in the case when you plug it into a computer.
