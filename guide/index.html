Rabbit r1 · MagSafe + Qi charging case · rev A

Bunnycage

A two-part printed case that snaps the r1 onto any MagSafe mount and charges it wirelessly. The r1 has no charging coil of its own, so the case carries one and feeds the USB-C port from a bay on the left edge.

  • Case, outside95.2 × 82.4 × 18.4 mm
  • Printed parts2, no supports
  • ChargingQi 5 V / 1 A
  • Plastic over magnets0.6 mm
  • Soldering2 wires

    Print settings

    Filesstl/bunnycage_body.stl, stl/bunnycage_lid.stl, and stl/bunnycage_fit_test.stl to try first, all in this project folder
    MaterialPETG. PLA softens near a warm coil.
    OrientationAs exported: body back-face down, lid top-face down
    Layer / nozzle0.2 mm / 0.4 mm
    Walls / infill3 perimeters, 20% infill. Most of the part is solid walls.
    SupportsNone. Lips are chamfered at 45°. The longest bridge is 23 mm, under the spine wall.
    First layersThe back skin is 3 layers. Print the first layer slowly so it is watertight.
